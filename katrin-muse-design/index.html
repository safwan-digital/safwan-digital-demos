The Studio

Nature, edited quietly.

Katrin & Muse Design is a Dubai interior studio led by founder Katerina Suleymanova. The work is nature-inspired contemporary luxury: beige and cream palettes, soft curves, and honest materials — marble, walnut, leather — composed the way Italian ateliers do it, for the way Dubai lives.
