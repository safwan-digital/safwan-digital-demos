What I do

About Kristina

One designer. No hand-offs.

I'm Kristina Rowe, the designer behind K'ROWE. My work starts with colour psychology — the simple idea that the colours around you change how you feel — and ends with art, the layer that makes a house truly yours.

Right now I'm renovating my own Abu Dhabi villa and sharing the whole process — the palettes, the mistakes, the wins — so you can see exactly how I work before you hire me.
