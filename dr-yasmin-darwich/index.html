About Dr. Yasmin

Skin care, considered
from the first conversation.

Dr. Yasmin Darwich is a Specialist Dermatologist with seven years of experience, practising at The Skin Lab in Al Bateen, Abu Dhabi. Her work spans medical dermatology and non-surgical aesthetic treatments — always beginning with a careful consultation.

She holds the Syrian Board in Dermatology and Venereology from Damascus University. Her approach is unhurried and evidence-led: listen first, examine carefully, and agree a plan that fits the person in front of her — not a standard protocol.

The consultation

Every plan starts
with a conversation.

Every treatment plan starts with a consultation. It is where your skin is understood properly — before anything is decided.

01

Listen & examine

Your history, your concerns and a careful examination of your skin, hair or scalp.

02

Diagnose

A clear explanation of what is happening with your skin, in plain language.

03

Personal plan

A treatment plan agreed together, tailored to you — medical, aesthetic, or both.

04

Review & aftercare

Follow-up and aftercare, so your plan is reviewed as your skin responds.

The consultation fee is deducted from your treatment. When you proceed with a treatment plan, the consultation fee is deducted from the treatment.
