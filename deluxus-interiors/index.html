01 — Studio

A studio that
builds what it draws.

Founded in Abu Dhabi in 2016, DELUXUS Interiors is a design and fit-out studio, not a brokerage. The people who draw your space are the people who build it.

We are a small, senior team. Every project is led by the founder, Arch. Shadi Abu Ajwa, and delivered by our own joiners, site engineers and project managers.

For more than twenty years in Abu Dhabi, Shadi has designed and delivered villas, offices, retail and commercial spaces — from the first concept sketch to the final handover, including authorities approvals. Nothing is handed to a third party and forgotten.

That is why our clients come back: one accountable team, one standard of finish, and a studio that stays on site until the last detail is right.

Arch. Shadi Abu Ajwa
Founder & Managing Director
