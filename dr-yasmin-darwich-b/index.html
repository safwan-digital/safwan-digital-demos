About the doctor

Careful medicine, honest advice.

Dr. Yasmin Darwich is a Specialist Dermatologist holding the Syrian Board in Dermatology & Venereology from Damascus University, with 7 years of clinical experience.

She treats medical skin conditions — acne, eczema, psoriasis, pigmentation and hair & scalp concerns — alongside non-surgical aesthetic treatments. Every plan starts with a consultation, and the consultation fee is deducted from your treatment.
